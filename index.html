About Me

I am a graphic and digital marketing designer based in Ann Arbor, MI. I completed my Bachelor's of Fine Arts and minor in Computer Science from the University of Michigan with a concentration in graphic and web design. Currently, I work as a Marketing and Communications Assistant at the Michigan Institute for Data & AI in Society where I am responsible for maintaining the institute's websites, designing print and digital marketing materials, and assisting with our various events. I'm passionate about all things design and believe that it is what brings a company to life. I'm always looking to expand my skills and knowledge to create exciting and fresh ideas.
