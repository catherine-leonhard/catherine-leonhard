Who Am I?

I am a graphic and digital marketing designer currently based in Auburn Hills, MI. I completed my Bachelor's of Fine Arts and minor in Computer Science from the University of Michigan with a concentration in graphic and web design. I work as a Marketing and Communications Assistant at the Michigan Institute for Data & AI in Society where I maintain, design, and launch the institute's websites and webpages, create print and digital marketing materials, and assist with our various events. I'm passionate about all things design and am always looking to expand my skills and knowledge to execute creative and innovative solutions.
